# GlobalPharm — Deck outline (7 slides)

Everything else is spoken. Slides carry the frame; you carry the value. Copilot-vs-lab and ROI math are handled **verbally** in objections, not on bullets.

### Slide 1 — Cover
- Title, your name, "Field Engineering." Nothing else. (You'll open by talking, not reading.)

### Slide 2 — Challenges at GlobalPharm
- A **recap**, not a claim. 3–4 bullets in *their* words from discovery.
- Placeholder bullets to overwrite live: uneven Copilot adoption on ~3,000 seats · validated-system changes wait weeks in evidence · legacy services nobody wants to touch · pressure to transform SDLC across business units.
- Footer: "Correct me — I'd rather be wrong now than at minute forty."

### Slide 3 — Where the pain actually is (the ladder)
- L1 (business): most expensive delay — e.g. time from data → submission, or batch release cycle.
- L2 (software on the critical path): the queues where change waits — validation evidence, review, legacy comprehension.
- L3 (the two places I'll attack): tie to demo. Get **explicit agreement on L2** before demoing.

### Slide 4 — Why Cursor (the compression)
- One idea: **governed velocity** — accelerate every station, human owns every gate, auditable end to end.
- Every number here is a **target vs your baseline**, not a benchmark or promise. Offer to replace placeholders with their discovery numbers.

### Slide 5 — Surfaces across your lifecycle
- Mirror their queues onto surfaces: Tab/Inline (flow) · Agent + Ask/Plan (understand + change) · Rules/Skills/MCP (org knowledge) · Bugbot (review) · Cloud Agents + Slack/Jira (async at scale).
- This is where you say "let me show you" and share screen.

### Slide 6 — Governed velocity (the champion's slide)
- The **named controls**, one line each: value-stream traceability · read-only blast-radius review · separation of duties · policy-as-config · quality gate · ITGC evidence + AI-authorship · automated review, human approval · **human holds the merge key**.
- This is the slide your champion forwards to their risk committee. Make it screenshot-clean.

### Slide 7 — Twelve weeks, guardrails first
- **Sequencing:** guardrails → enablement → expansion.
- Baseline day-zero on four lenses: adoption · flow · quality/safety · experience/trust.
- **Deliberately defer** autonomous agents in CI to phase two (say it out loud).
- End with the ask: pilot scope + security working session + owners and dates.
