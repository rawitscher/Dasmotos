# The 90-second point of view

You *will* be asked, in some form: "What's your take on AI in the enterprise?" Rehearsed but not recited.

**0–15s — the shift**
> Every company is adopting AI coding. The winners **govern** it — they don't ban it and they don't turn it loose.

**15–45s — the unit**
> The metric that matters is **throughput per governed engineer across the value stream** — not seat cost, not tokens, and definitely not lines of AI-written code. In a regulated shop, velocity you can't audit isn't velocity; it's risk.

**45–70s — the mechanism**
> Cursor accelerates every station from ticket to PR, and a **human owns every gate** — plan approval and merge. AI proposes; people approve. On a validated system that's not a nice-to-have, it's the whole game.

**70–90s — the proof + control**
> It's auditable end to end: named controls, audit logs, model and repo allowlists — the things a risk committee can actually sign. That's the difference between a demo that wows and a platform your change board approves.

---

**If compressed to one line:** *"Govern the velocity — accelerate every station, keep a human on every gate, and make the whole thing auditable."*
