# Objection drills — say it out loud

Practice 20-second answers. Then a 60-second version with a question back.

---

### Budget / “We already bought Copilot”

**20s:** “Totally fair — you shouldn’t pay twice for the same job. The question is whether Copilot is covering *multi-service delivery, review capacity, and governed agents*. Most shops find Copilot strong at the caret and thin across the SDLC. Let’s pick two real Northstar tasks and score both tools.”

**Return question:** “If Copilot already solved onboarding into your monolith, we shouldn’t be here — where does it still fall down?”

---

### Risk / “Code can’t leave our boundary”

**20s:** “Privacy Mode enforced org-wide: your code isn’t used for training by us or model providers, with ZDR agreements. On Enterprise you also get model allowlists, MCP allowlists, repo controls, hooks, audit logs, and spend caps. Happy to do a dedicated session with your security architects and walk trust.cursor.com.”

**Return question:** “Is the hard requirement no egress at all, or controlled egress with contractual ZDR + admin policy?”

---

### IDE lock-in

**20s:** “Cursor is VS Code–native, so that migration is usually hours, not months. For JetBrains-heavy teams we plan a dual track — agent/CLI paths and no big-bang mandate. We win on the workflows first, editor second.”

**Return question:** “What percentage of productive coding hours are VS Code vs IntelliJ today?”

---

### Lab-led build

**20s:** “You can absolutely call Claude or GPT from an internal tool. You’re then owning context retrieval, tool use, evals, IDE UX, secret handling, audit, and support for 5,000 people. That’s a platform team’s multi-year roadmap — Cursor’s product is that harness, multi-model, with the control plane already built.”

**Return question:** “Do you have a funded team to build and staff an internal AI SDLC platform for the next two years?”

---

### Legacy COBOL/VB6

**20s:** “Cursor won’t teleport the mainframe to Kubernetes. It *will* help the humans who still have to wrap, document, and strangler-fig those systems — and Rules let you capture tribal knowledge so it isn’t stuck in one retiree’s head.”

**Return question:** “Are you modernizing in place, wrapping, or rewriting — and where is the knowledge bottleneck?”

---

### ROI skepticism

**20s:** “Let’s not argue benchmarks in the abstract. Design a 4–6 week pilot with three teams, baseline cycle time and PR rework, enable analytics/AI code tracking, and decide with evidence. If it doesn’t move your metrics, you shouldn’t expand.”

**Return question:** “Which two metrics does Finance already believe for developer productivity?”
